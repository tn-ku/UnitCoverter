Unit Converter

A simple unit converter for energy and X-ray diffraction (XRD) with NO WARRANTY and for private use only. Convert between eV, Hartree, kJ mol⁻¹, cm⁻¹, nm and K, find 2θ, d and Q from an X-ray wavelength or energy, work out the mass (mg), amount (mmol) and concentration (mM) of a reagent solution, or calculate the density of a crystal from its chemical formula and unit cell.

Type a value in any field. The other units update at once.

1. X-ray source

Set the wavelength or the energy. Either one updates the other.

2. Diffraction

Type 2θ, d, or Q. The other two update at once (first-order reflection, n = 1).

1. Reagent and solution

Set the molecular weight and the volume of the solution.

2. Mass, amount and concentration

Type the mass, the amount, or the concentration. The other two update at once.

1. Chemical formula

Enter a formula such as H2O, Ca(OH)2 or CuSO4·5H2O.

2. Unit cell

Enter the cell parameters and Z (the number of formula units per cell).

3. Result

Calculated in your browser. Constants h, c, e, NA and kB are SI-exact; the Hartree energy is CODATA 2022.

Energy: wavenumber and wavelength are converted with E = hc × wavenumber and E = hc / wavelength. Temperature is E / kB (the molar energy divided by the gas constant R).

XRD: λ = 2d sinθ, Q = 4π sinθ / λ = 2π / d, λ(Å) = 12.3984198 / E(keV). Preset wavelengths are typical tabulated values.

Reagent: amount (mmol) = mass (mg) / molecular weight (g/mol) = concentration (mM) × volume (mL) / 1000.

Crystal: V = abc√(1 − cos²α − cos²β − cos²γ + 2 cosα cosβ cosγ), density = Z × M / (NA × V). Atomic weights are IUPAC abbreviated standard values (elements up to Fm; the mass number of the longest-lived isotope is used where no stable isotope exists). Formulas may use parentheses and hydrate dots, e.g. CuSO4·5H2O.
