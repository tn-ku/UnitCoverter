Unit Converter

A simple unit converter for energy and X-ray diffraction (XRD) with NO WARRANTY and for private use only. Convert between eV, Hartree, kJ mol⁻¹, cm⁻¹ and nm, or find 2θ, d and Q from an X-ray wavelength or energy. Everything runs in your browser.

Type a value in any field. The other units update at once.

1. X-ray source

Set the wavelength or the energy. Either one updates the other.

2. Diffraction

Type 2θ, d, or Q. The other two update at once (first-order reflection, n = 1).

Calculated in your browser. Constants h, c, e and NA are SI-exact; the Hartree energy is CODATA 2022.

Energy: wavenumber and wavelength are converted with E = hc × wavenumber and E = hc / wavelength.

XRD: λ = 2d sinθ, Q = 4π sinθ / λ = 2π / d, λ(Å) = 12.3984198 / E(keV). Preset wavelengths are typical tabulated values.
